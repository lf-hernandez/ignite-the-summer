The Format

Enlighten us, but make it quick

Ignite is a talk format built for momentum. Your slides advance automatically, whether you're ready or not. It's exhilarating, a little terrifying, and the most fun you'll ever have presenting.

5 Minutes total

Every talk is exactly five minutes. No overtime and no Q&A, so every second counts.

20 Slides

Exactly twenty slides. Big visuals beat bullet points, so keep them punchy.

15 Seconds per slide

Slides auto-advance every 15 seconds. The clock is your co-presenter.

1 Big idea

One story, one lesson, one obsession. Go deep instead of wide and light the spark.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is an Ignite talk?

A five-minute talk with exactly 20 slides that advance automatically every 15 seconds. You can't skip ahead or go back, so it rewards a tight story and big visuals. More on the format.

Do I need my slides ready to submit?

No. Just send a title and a couple of sentences about your idea. Accepted speakers get slide specs and a deadline by email.

Can I submit more than one talk?

Yes. Fill out the form once for each idea.

I've never given a talk before. Should I still apply?

Absolutely. Five minutes is the perfect first talk, and first-time speakers get help rehearsing before the big night.

Does my talk have to be technical?

Not at all. Career stories, lessons from failure, side projects and hobbies all make great Ignite talks. If it would make a room of developers lean in, pitch it.

Can I talk about my company's product?

You can talk about something you built or a problem your team solved, but sales pitches won't be selected. Teach us something, tell us a story, or make us laugh.

When will I hear back?

By Monday, December 7. Everyone who submits hears back, accepted or not.

How much does it cost to attend?

Ticket details are coming soon. Check the Attend section or join our Discord for updates.

Is this event for all ages?

Yes, everyone is welcome.

I need an accommodation. Who do I tell?

Mention it in your talk submission, or message the event organizer (rwx0x5a) on Discord. We'll work with you and the venue.

I have a question that isn't here.

Ask in our Discord and an organizer will get back to you.
